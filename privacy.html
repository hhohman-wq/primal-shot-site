Primal Shot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Developer: Hayden Hohman · Contact: hhohman13@gmail.com

Primal Shot ("the game") is a casual arcade game. This policy explains what information the game and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (gold, upgrades, Field Journal, settings and purchases) is saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not ask for your name, email or account.

Advertising

The free version shows ads, including optional reward videos. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. On iPhone you are asked for permission before the advertising identifier is used for tracking, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Buying "Remove Ads", the Starter Pack or the Hunter's Pass turns off between-run ads; optional reward videos stay available.

Analytics

The game does not use a separate analytics service. Google AdMob and RevenueCat (below) receive the limited data described in their sections.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and subscriptions so we can deliver them and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase history for this game, your device type and your country. It does not receive your name or email.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your traps are full). You can turn them off in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Limit ad tracking in your device settings, or decline consent when asked.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the game or using Settings > Reset progress.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may request access to or deletion of data held by our partners. Contact us and we will help, or contact the partner directly through their privacy policy.

Partners' policies

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
